Cat Games: Mini World Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

Trung Sam Creative Design Co Ltd provides the iOS game Cat Games: Mini World. The app plays offline and does not require an account. The app does not send personal information or gameplay data to us or our service providers. It does not contain ads, tracking or analytics SDKs.

Data stored on your device

The app stores game progress, scores, language and sound preferences, unlocked games, and the shared coin balance on your device. That data is retained while the app and its data are installed. To remove it from the device, use Delete App in iOS Settings or on the Home Screen.

Contacting us

If you contact us using the Yoilit Game Studio support form, we use the details you choose to send to answer your request. Use the same form to ask about privacy or to request deletion of a support message.
